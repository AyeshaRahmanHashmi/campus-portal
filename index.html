Welcome to the KICSIT University Student Portal

Course details, faculty contacts, notices and assignment deadlines have until now been published in separate places. The Student Portal collects them in a single site that works on a phone, a tablet or a desktop computer.

Use the dashboard for a summary of your semester, the course catalogue to look up instructors and credit hours, and the task manager to record coursework deadlines. Tasks and preferences you save here stay in your own browser.
